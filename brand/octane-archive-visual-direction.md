# Octane Archive — Visual-first pilot

## Direction

Faceless automotive edits led by real moving footage. Every frame should show a car, track, tires, cockpit, smoke, or racing action. Cut to the soundtrack's beat, keep playback smooth, use occasional impact flashes and punch-ins, and preserve engine/tire sound quietly under rights-cleared music. Use tight, high-contrast title hooks that disappear quickly. No fact-led narration, generic abstract backdrops, or long explanatory captions.

## First experiment

Three 10.2-second vertical edits use Pexels-licensed racing footage and test night drift, wet-track racing, and burnout/drift. Each has five shots, a short opening title, restrained Signal House lime accents, natural track audio, and a small Octane Archive footer. Cuts use short 0.2-second transitions selected to suit each edit, with audio fades at the beginning and end. Horizontal source shots retain the full car and track in a centered widescreen window; vertical source footage fills the canvas. Exports are 720×1280 drafts.

The earlier narration-led “First Stories” batch is superseded. Use this visual edit pilot as the current direction.

## Publishing notes

- Keep the account clearly editorial; do not imply an endorsement by any visible car maker, event, or driver.
- Add platform-native music when posting if a music-led version is wanted. The local drafts keep source audio where available.
