# Signal House

**Find the signal. Make it matter.**

Signal House is Clipflow's internal creative studio: a shared team-of-one operating system for making content for Clipflow, MStudio, and experimental media brands. It can become a client-facing creative company later without changing the core identity.

## Identity

- **Position:** Independent creative studio for clear, memorable digital storytelling.
- **Promise:** Turn an idea into a story people notice and remember.
- **Personality:** Thoughtful, editorial, assured, curious, precise.
- **Voice:** Clear and specific. Use evidence. Avoid agency jargon, empty superlatives, and performative hype.
- **Tagline:** Find the signal. Make it matter.
- **Descriptor:** Strategy / Story / Studio.
- **Mark:** An open architectural frame with an inset signal path. The frame represents shaping; the nested path represents an idea becoming a story.

## Visual system

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | Main dark field | `#101311` |
| Bone | Main light field | `#F4F1E9` |
| Signal | Accent / callout | `#C9F05A` |
| Slate | Secondary copy | `#899087` |
| Paper | Light surface | `#E6E2D8` |

Use a restrained editorial grid, generous negative space, strong typographic hierarchy, subtle paper grain, and the signal accent sparingly. Keep product brands independent: Clipflow retains lime-on-charcoal, and MStudio retains electric blue.

## Typography and image direction

- **Headlines:** Space Grotesk, weight 500–700.
- **Body:** Inter, weight 400–500.
- **Labels:** DM Mono, uppercase and tracked.
- **Image direction:** Documentary product and process footage; purposeful crops; deep shadows; natural material texture; one clear focal point. Avoid generic stock-photo agency tropes.

## Usage

Use the icon without the wordmark at small sizes. Keep the logo bone on ink or ink on bone; use signal green for the frame path, not as a large text color. Do not apply the Signal House palette over client/product brands unless a co-branded piece calls for it.
