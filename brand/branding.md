## Content pillars (weekly rotation)

1. **Build in public** — what shipped, metrics, lessons. Format: screen
   recordings + honest captions. (Trust engine.)
2. **Before/after** — bland idea text vs finished Clipflow video side by
   side. (Demo engine.)
3. **Creator playbooks** — hooks, retention cuts, caption styles, posting
   cadence. One tactic per post. (Value engine.)
4. **Client proof (MSTUDIO)** — finished sites + the videos that sell them.
   (Revenue engine.)
5. **Dogfood** — every MSTUDIO/Clipflow announcement video is itself made
   with Clipflow, captioned as such. (Proof engine.)

## Visual rules

- 9:16 everything; captions chunked ≤3 words, punch word in blue.
- Dark backgrounds, blue accent bar, lime reserved for CTAs and wins.
- Never: em-dashes in copy, pill labels, off-topic stock (every asset
  visually audited before entering the pool).
