# Featured Clipflow and MStudio videos

The first two bespoke vertical clips in batch `batch-3329024d-d228-4a1a-b2dc-5b3110fca943` are defined in `scripts/prepare-featured-clips.ts`. That script is the source for copy, narration, scene weights, captions, background prompts, music, and sound cues. It writes the creative treatment into each clip manifest and regenerates local Kokoro speech. The `bg.prompt` and 12-character prompt digest travel with each scene; the preparation script refuses to reuse an image when its prompt changes.

The eight current scene images were generated with Codex's built-in image generation tool, then saved under `public/scene-backgrounds/`. The four Clipflow scenes use a lime spark, assembling film frames, a complete five-node film spine, and a dawn horizon. The four MStudio scenes use a blank price tag in blue fog, a blueprint/storefront, three blank glass cards, and a door of blue light. All prompts require a 9:16 cinematic still, dark negative space, one brand accent, and **no generated text, logos, or numbers**. Remotion supplies all typography and prices. The script checks that all images exist; after changing a prompt, generate a replacement still at its path and run the preparation script with `--accept-new-images` to update the cached digest.

MStudio's three offers follow [its pricing page](https://www.mstudioweb.pt/pricing): Speed Site €299, AI Automation Build €449, and Brand + Website System €699. The €699 offer is not described as a bundle. The video CTA says “link in bio”; the post caption may include the URL. Clipflow's CTA is “Type your idea. Get the video,” with “Follow Clipflow for updates” in the post caption until a verified destination exists.

At 1080×1920, keep primary text at least 90 px from the sides, captions around y=1090 (57% of frame height), and essential elements above y=1450. Each scene uses a gentle image push/drift, grain and scrim, a spring/mask headline reveal, and a distinct diagram or price-card sequence. Phrase captions highlight only semantic words. The score is a locally synthesized, reusable bed with transition whooshes and node/card pops. Voices are normalized and measured near −14 LUFS; music stays below speech. The production target is one compact narration with less than 1.5 seconds of tail silence per clip.

To update these two clips, edit the story data, provide fresh stills if prompts changed, run `npx tsx scripts/prepare-featured-clips.ts`, then approve and queue with `npx tsx scripts/render-featured-clips.ts`. Review video frames and audio before exporting the batch ZIP. The other eight clips in this batch retain their existing treatments. Future experiments can replace selected stills with 4–5 second loops and add a PT-PT MStudio version after this still-based cut is approved.
