# CLIPFLOW Remotion editorial standard

The cinematic treatment approved on 2026-09-28 becomes the starting point for every new Remotion Studio clip. Existing rendered clips keep their current manifests and exports. A new batch clip opens as a four-scene vertical storyboard with its brand palette, a curated text-free still for each scene when available, Ken Burns movement, grain and scrim, spring-revealed display type, a safe centered caption area, a quiet music bed, and transition sound cues. The batch still requires review before rendering. Editors can replace the curated stills with topic-specific images and add a visual sequence such as animated steps or cards.

The storyboard is manifest-driven: scene copy supplies the display headline; optional scene `visual` data supplies diagram labels. The existing Clipflow spine and MStudio pricing treatments remain valid. All crucial copy stays above y=1450 on a 1080×1920 frame, with captions centered around y=1090 and a minimum 40 px text size. Generated imagery never contains text or logos. Narration is normalized near −14 LUFS and video length follows speech with less than 1.5 seconds of tail. Phrase captions follow narration without highlighting filler words. The code preserves the original brand palettes: Clipflow lime/black and MStudio electric blue/black.

New batch creation assigns this storyboard automatically. A supported append operation adds up to the 20-clip batch limit, allowing small topic-led additions to an existing batch. A clip may carry a dated primary-source reference in its creative manifest and post copy. Research claims must be checked before copy is written; the renderer itself does not fetch trends or make factual claims.

The first acceptance examples are two English 9:16 clips added to the current mixed-brand batch. Clipflow explains YouTube's 2026-09-23 [Shorts Series rollout](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-creators-shorts-series-tv-features/) with a five-episode arc. MStudio responds to Google's [AI Search website guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features/) with three grounded basics: crawlable pages, useful text, and clear internal links. Each gets four new stills, distinct visual motion, phrase captions, voice, music and cues, a post caption with the primary source, a reviewed MP4/cover, and inclusion in a refreshed ZIP. Neither clip promises ranking or that Shorts Series is available to every creator.

Verification: new batch clips use the storyboard by default; prior featured manifests still render; the append operation enforces capacity and identity; both MP4s have audible audio and visually safe frames; tests and production build pass; the export manifest includes all 12 ready clips.
